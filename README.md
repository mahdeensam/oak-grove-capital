# Oak Grove Capital: the 77 companies

A single-page, self-contained reference for the 77 holdings in Oak Grove Capital's
Q2 2026 13F, built for someone who wants to understand what these businesses
actually are rather than just read tickers.

**Live site:** https://mahdeensam.github.io/oak-grove-capital/

## What it does

- **The table** lists all 77 holdings across 10 sectors, with position value,
  price a year ago versus now, what each company sells, its main product, and
  who buys it.
- **Click a company name** for a plain-English explanation of the business:
  where it came from, how it actually makes money, and why it sits in this
  portfolio.
- **Click a main product** for what the thing is, how it works, and how it
  differs from what competitors sell. Covers the jargon directly, from HBM and
  EUV lithography to how a regulated utility earns its return.
- **Flashcards** quiz you on all of it: 385 cards across six question types,
  filterable by sector, with self-grading and a review round for the ones you
  miss.

## Notes

- One file, no dependencies, no build step, no network requests. Open
  `index.html` in any browser and it works offline.
- Follows the viewer's light or dark theme.
- Independent notes compiled from public 13F filings. Not affiliated with or
  endorsed by Oak Grove Capital, and not investment advice.
- Competitive claims reflect a point in time and are worth re-checking against
  current sources.
